## 📰 News & Media Monitoring

[← Back to Index](../README.md)

### News Aggregators
- [Google News](https://news.google.com/) — Aggregated news across global sources  
- [Yahoo News](https://news.yahoo.com/) — Mainstream news aggregation and coverage  
- [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/) — European and international news service, with live TV  
- [AllSides](https://www.allsides.com/unbiased-balanced-news) — News aggregation with political bias rating, showing left, center, and right coverage side by side  
- [Ground News](https://ground.news/) — Compares how news stories are covered across the political spectrum (subscription with free trial)  
- [NewsNow](https://www.newsnow.co.uk/) — Breaking news aggregation from thousands of sources by region and topic, refreshed every five minutes  

### News Agencies & Business News
- [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/) — International newswire service  
- [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/) — Business and global news (commercial)  

### Media Search Engines
- [GDELT Project](https://www.gdeltproject.org/) — Global database of events, language, and tone in news media, monitoring broadcast, print, and web news in over 100 languages  
- [Media Cloud](https://mediacloud.org/) — Open-source research tool for analyzing how news and information spread online  
- [Event Registry](https://eventregistry.org/) — AI-driven news aggregation and analysis from 150,000+ publishers worldwide  

### Adverse and Risk-focused Media
- [Adverse Media Search](https://www.no-nonsense-intel.com/adverse-media-search-tool) — Search for adverse media reports on entities  
- [Dow Jones Risk & Compliance](https://professional.dowjones.com/risk-compliance/) — Adverse media and watchlists (commercial)  
- [Nexis Newsdesk (LexisNexis)](https://www.lexisnexis.com/en-us/products/newsdesk.page) — Professional news and social media monitoring with analytics (commercial)  

### Source Reliability & Fact-Checking
- [Media Bias/Fact Check](https://mediabiasfactcheck.com/) — Searchable database that rates news sources for political bias and factual accuracy  
- [Google Fact Check Explorer](https://toolbox.google.com/factcheck/explorer) — Search fact-checks published by fact-checking organizations worldwide  
- [EUvsDisinfo](https://euvsdisinfo.eu/) — EEAS East StratCom Task Force project with a database of debunked pro-Kremlin disinformation cases  
- [IFCN at Poynter](https://www.poynter.org/ifcn/) — International Fact-Checking Network, with a code of principles and a global network of fact-checkers  
- [Snopes](https://www.snopes.com/) — Fact-checking site with a large archive of investigations into viral claims  

### Broadcast and Live Monitoring
- [LiveNewsNow](https://www.livenewsnow.com/) — Live streaming of US news channels  
- [Internet Archive TV News Archive](https://archive.org/details/tv) — Search closed captions of 3 million+ US broadcasts and cite or compare short clips  
- [EarthCam](https://www.earthcam.com/) — Live webcams from around the world  

### Alerts & Change Monitoring
- [Google Alerts](https://www.google.com/alerts) — Email alerts for new web content matching your keywords  
- [Talkwalker Alerts](https://www.talkwalker.com/alerts) — Free Google Alerts alternative for web, news, blog, and social mentions, delivered by email, RSS, or Slack  
- [Visualping](https://visualping.io/) — Monitors websites for changes and alerts you by email, SMS, or Slack (free personal plan)  
- [Inoreader](https://www.inoreader.com/) — Feed reader that collects websites, social feeds, and newsletters into one stream (freemium)  
- [Feedly](https://feedly.com/) — Feed reader and threat intelligence platform for collecting and analyzing news, vulnerabilities, and threat actor activity (30-day trial)  

### Social Media & Trend Tracking 
- [BuzzSumo](https://buzzsumo.com/) — Content monitoring and social trend analysis (paid subscription, free trial)  
- [Mention](https://mention.com/en/) — Monitor mentions across news and social media (paid, free trial)
