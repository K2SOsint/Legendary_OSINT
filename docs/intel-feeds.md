## 📡 Intelligence Feeds

[← Back to Index](../README.md)

### Community Threat Feeds
- [ThreatFox](https://threatfox.abuse.ch/) — Community-driven IOC feed  
- [URLhaus](https://urlhaus.abuse.ch/) — Malicious URL tracker  
- [Feodo Tracker](https://feodotracker.abuse.ch/) — Banking trojan command and control trackers; datasets are currently empty following law-enforcement takedowns (Emotet 2021, Operation Endgame 2024)  
- [MalwareBazaar](https://bazaar.abuse.ch/) — Malware sample and hash sharing platform  
- [Abuse.ch SSLBL](https://sslbl.abuse.ch/) — Blocklist of malicious SSL certificates and JA3 fingerprints used by botnet C2s  
- [YARAify](https://yaraify.abuse.ch/) — Scan files against a large repository of YARA rules to detect malware  
- [Abuse.ch Hunting](https://hunting.abuse.ch/) — Search across all abuse.ch platforms with a single query  
- [AlienVault OTX (LevelBlue)](https://otx.alienvault.com/) — Open threat exchange community  
- [TweetFeed](https://tweetfeed.live/) — IOCs shared by the infosec community on X/Twitter, updated every 15 minutes  
- [Pulsedive](https://pulsedive.com/) — Free platform to search, scan, and enrich IOCs  

### Commercial and Specialized Feeds
- [Spamhaus](https://www.spamhaus.org/) — Blocklists for IPs, domains, and spam sources  
- [AbuseIPDB](https://www.abuseipdb.com/) — Community-reported IP abuse database  
- [MISP Project Feeds](https://www.misp-project.org/feeds/) — Default and community feed list for the MISP threat-sharing platform, includes the CIRCL OSINT feed  
- [Cisco Talos Intelligence Center](https://talosintelligence.com/reputation_center) — IP and domain reputation lookup and threat data from Cisco Talos  
- [IBM X-Force Exchange](https://exchange.xforce.ibmcloud.com/) — Threat intelligence sharing platform for researching threats and collaborating with peers  
- [SANS Internet Storm Center](https://isc.sans.edu/) — Community-driven threat monitoring with daily diaries, podcasts, and real-time threat data  

### IP & Network Blocklists
- [CINS Army List](https://cinsscore.com/) — Free list of IP addresses flagged as malicious by a global network of Sentinel IPS sensors  
- [IPsum](https://github.com/stamparm/ipsum) — Daily feed of bad IPs with blacklist hit scores, aggregated from 30+ public blocklists  
- [FireHOL blocklist-ipsets](https://github.com/firehol/blocklist-ipsets) — Dynamically updated collection of IP blocklists aggregated from multiple sources  
- [blocklist.de](https://lists.blocklist.de/) — Blocklists of attacking IPs organized by service, such as SSH, FTP, and mail  
- [Maltrail](https://github.com/stamparm/maltrail) — Network traffic detection system that identifies communication with known malicious infrastructure using public blocklists  

### Phishing Feeds
- [PhishTank](https://phishtank.org/) — Community phishing URL repository operated by Cisco Talos (overlaps phishing but also feed source)  
- [OpenPhish](https://openphish.com/) — Automated phishing intelligence feed  
- [Phishing.Database](https://github.com/mitchellkrogza/Phishing.Database) — Open-source database of phishing domains, URLs, and IPs, validated with PyFunceble  

### Ransomware & Threat Reporting
- [Ransomware.live](https://www.ransomware.live/) — Leak site monitoring platform that tracks ransomware groups, their victims, and activity  
- [The DFIR Report](https://thedfirreport.com/) — Detailed reports on observed intrusions, with actionable intelligence and detection guidance  

### Vulnerability Intelligence
- [CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities Catalog](https://www.cisa.gov/known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog) — US government catalog of vulnerabilities under active exploitation  
- [VulnCheck KEV](https://vulncheck.com/kev) — Known exploited vulnerabilities catalog, free for registered community members  
- [EPSS](https://www.first.org/epss/) — Daily probability scores estimating whether a CVE will be exploited in the wild within 30 days  
- [NVD](https://nvd.nist.gov/) — NIST's National Vulnerability Database of CVE records, scores, and references  
- [CVE.org](https://www.cve.org/) — Official CVE Program site and record search  
- [OSV](https://osv.dev/) — Distributed vulnerability database for open-source software  
- [GitHub Advisory Database](https://github.com/advisories) — Searchable database of security advisories across software ecosystems  
- [IntheWild](https://inthewild.io/) — Tracks actively exploited CVEs and exploitation data  

### Attack Surface and Internet Scanning
- [GreyNoise](https://www.greynoise.io/) — Contextual intelligence for internet scanners  
- [Shodan Monitor](https://monitor.shodan.io/) — Real-time alerts on unexpected exposure of devices within your network ranges  
- [Censys Search and Data](https://censys.com/) — Enriched dataset of hosts and certificates  
- [urlscan.io](https://urlscan.io/) — Scans and analyzes URLs for suspicious or malicious content
