### Guides and Books
- [Open Source Intelligence Techniques (Michael Bazzell)](https://inteltechniques.com/) — Comprehensive OSINT book and toolkit  
- [Bellingcat OSINT Guides](https://www.bellingcat.com/resources/) — Investigative journalism and OSINT techniques  
- [OSINT Techniques (Sector035’s Curated Lists)](https://sector035.nl/) — Weekly curated OSINT links and guides  
- [Verification Handbook](https://verificationhandbook.com/) — Guide to verifying digital content and user-generated media, written by journalists from major newsrooms  
- [GIJN Resource Center](https://gijn.org/resource/) — Library of 2,000+ guides, tipsheets, and databases for investigative journalists, in 14 languages  
- [Psychology of Intelligence Analysis (Richards J. Heuer)](https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/static/Pyschology-of-Intelligence-Analysis.pdf) — Classic CIA Center for the Study of Intelligence book on cognitive bias and analytic reasoning  
- [Handbook of Collective Intelligence (MIT)](https://cci.mit.edu/cichapterlinks/) — Research and methodology (academic)

- [DataExpert](https://dataexpert.eu) — Training, software, and services for OSINT, cybercrime, and cryptocurrency investigations in Europe  
- [OSINT Dojo](https://www.osintdojo.com/) — Free structured learning path for OSINT  
- [OSINT Curious](https://www.osintcurio.us/) — Blog, podcast, and tutorials (archived but valuable)  
- [Trace Labs OSINT VM & CTFs](https://www.tracelabs.org/) — Virtual machines and OSINT competitions  
- [Quiztime](https://twitter.com/quiztime) — Daily OSINT challenges on Twitter/X  
- [I-Intelligence](https://i-intelligence.eu/) — European provider of intelligence training for OSINT, analysis, and more  
- [Bellingcat Workshops](https://www.bellingcat.com/workshops/) — Paid training on open source investigations  
- [Kase Scenarios](https://kasescenarios.com/) — Immersive free and paid OSINT scenarios to learn and train skills  
- [Gralhix](https://gralhix.com/) — Free OSINT exercises, tutorials, and articles from beginner to expert level  
- [Maltego Academy](https://academy.maltego.com/) — Online learning platform with training for investigations in Maltego  
- [OpenSecurityTraining2](https://ost2.fyi/) — Free, open-source cybersecurity classes organized into structured learning pathways
