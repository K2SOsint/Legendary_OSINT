## 🚆 Railway & Train OSINT

### Reference Maps
- [Global Railway Map Reference](https://www.openrailwaymap.org/) — OpenStreetMap-based map of the world's railway infrastructure  
- [Dutch Railway Map](https://spoorkaart.mwnn.nl/) — Visualizes actual train movement in the Netherlands based on live travel information  
- [Swiss Railways Network](https://maps.vasile.ch/transit-sbb/) — Animated map based on official SBB (Swiss Federal Railways) timetables  
- [Slovakia Rail Map](https://mapa.zsr.sk/) — Interactive map of the Slovak rail network  

### Live Train Tracking by Country
- [Zugradar (ÖBB Scotty)](https://fahrplan.oebb.at/webapp/?showLivemap=true) — Live train map for Austria, built into ÖBB's journey planner  
- [VR Trains Finland](https://www.vr.fi/junat-kartalla) — Real-time positions of trains across Finland  
- [TRAVIC / geOps Mobility Portal](https://mobility.portal.geops.io/) — Live positions of public transport vehicles, including trains, worldwide  
- [OVzoeker NL](https://ovzoeker.nl/) — Live public transport vehicle tracking in the Netherlands  
- [PKP Portal Pasażera](https://portalpasazera.pl/MapaOL) — Live train map and movement simulation for Poland  
- [UK Train Map (traintimes.org.uk)](https://traintimes.org.uk/map/#bhm) — Shows trains currently approaching a chosen UK station  
- [Trainradar Track-Trace](https://track-trace.live/trainradar/) — Free real-time train positions for the US (Amtrak), Europe, the UK, and India  
- [TrainTrack Hungary](https://traintrack.hu/?lang=en) — Live positions, delays, and station boards for Hungarian trains, built after MÁV shut down its own Vonatinfó service in June 2025  
- [Realtime Trains UK](https://www.realtimetrains.co.uk/) — Independent real-time departure/arrival boards for the Great British rail network  
- [TrainTracker24](https://www.traintracker24.com/) — "Flightradar for rail" launched in August 2026, showing estimated train positions, delays, and cancellations in Belgium, France, Italy, Spain, and Luxembourg (basic features free, positions are estimated from open operator data rather than GPS)  
- [Bahn Experte](https://bahn.expert/) — Community-built companion for Deutsche Bahn that aggregates train, station, and delay information from multiple sources  
- [RailRadar India](https://railradar.in/) — Real-time GPS locations of Indian Railways trains, with live station boards  

### Open Data & Transit Feeds
- [Transitland](https://www.transit.land/) — Open APIs, datasets, and historical archives of bus, train, subway, and ferry feeds from agencies worldwide  
- [MobilityDatabase](https://mobilitydatabase.org/) — Global catalog of 6,000+ GTFS, GTFS-Realtime, and GBFS public transport feeds from 99+ countries  
- [Open Rail Data Wiki](https://wiki.openraildata.com/) — Community documentation of the open data available from the rail industry in Great Britain  
- [Network Rail Open Data Feeds](https://www.networkrail.co.uk/who-we-are/transparency-and-ethics/transparency/open-data-feeds/) — Operational data feeds from Britain's infrastructure manager, such as train movements and schedules  
- [Rail Delivery Group Data](https://www.raildeliverygroup.com/our-services/rail-data.html) — UK rail industry data feeds (fares, timetables, National Rail Enquiries)  
- [Data.gov Railroad Datasets](https://catalog.data.gov/dataset?tags=railroad) — US government open datasets tagged railroad  
- [North American Rail Network Lines](https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/north-american-rail-network-lines3) — FRA geographic database of rail lines, ownership, and trackage rights across the US, Canada, and Mexico, updated quarterly  
- [BC Canada Railway Track Dataset](https://catalogue.data.gov.bc.ca/dataset/railway-track-line) — British Columbia government railway track line dataset  

### Infrastructure, Safety & Investigations
- [RINF (EU Register of Infrastructure)](https://rinf.era.europa.eu/) — European Union Agency for Railways register of infrastructure characteristics of the EU rail network (service available during daily operating hours)  
- [European Union Agency for Railways (ERA)](https://www.era.europa.eu/) — EU agency for railway interoperability and safety, with vehicle authorisations and safety publications  
- [Federal Railroad Administration](https://railroads.dot.gov/) — US rail regulator, with safety data, accident reporting, and investigations  
- [US DOT Railroads](https://www.transportation.gov/railroads) — US Department of Transportation railroad programs and the Federal Railroad Administration  
- [Rail Accident Investigation Branch (RAIB)](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/rail-accident-investigation-branch) — UK independent investigator of railway accidents, with published investigation reports  
- [Dutch Safety Board](https://www.onderzoeksraad.nl/en/) — Dutch independent investigator of accidents across rail, aviation, shipping, and other sectors  

### Industry & Directories
- [Railway Technology Company A-Z](https://www.railway-technology.com/company-a-z) — Directory of railway industry suppliers and contractors  
- [Rail Webcams](https://railwebcams.net/) — Directory of 1,180+ live railway webcams worldwide  
- [Interrail Trains in Europe](https://www.interrail.com/en/plan-your-trip/tips-and-tricks/trains-europe) — Country-by-country directory of European railways and train types  
- [Transport Scotland Public Transport](https://www.transport.gov.scot/public-transport) — Scottish government rail, bus, ferry, and air transport information
