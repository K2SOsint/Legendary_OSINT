## 🎥 Video OSINT

[← Back to Index](../README.md)

### Video Verification
- [InVID/WeVerify](https://www.invid-project.eu/tools-and-services/invid-verification-plugin/) — Browser plugin for video and image verification, with keyframe extraction, reverse image search, and forensic filters; now maintained by AFP Medialab as "InVID WeVerify VeraAI" ([GitHub](https://github.com/AFP-Medialab/invid-verification-plugin))  

### Video Metadata & Processing
- [MediaInfo](https://mediaarea.net/en/MediaInfo) — Displays technical metadata and tags for video and audio files  
- [FFmpeg](https://ffmpeg.org/) — Cross-platform tool to record, convert, and stream audio and video; useful for extracting frames and clips  

### Video Search
- [Filmot](https://filmot.com/) — Search within YouTube subtitles, captions, and transcripts across 1.7+ billion indexed videos  

### Video Download and Archiving
- [yt-dlp](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp) — Download and archive YouTube and other videos  
- [4K Video Downloader](https://www.4kdownload.com/products/videodownloader-42) — Cross-platform video downloader (freemium)  
- [Cobalt](https://cobalt.tools/) — Web tool for saving media from various online services, without ads or tracking  
- [gallery-dl](https://codeberg.org/mikf/gallery-dl) — Command-line tool to download image galleries and collections from many image hosting sites (development moved from GitHub to Codeberg)  
- [Streamlink](https://streamlink.github.io/) — Command-line utility that extracts live streams from various services and pipes them to a video player or file  

### Streaming and Broadcast Analysis
- [TwitchTracker](https://twitchtracker.com/) — Analytics and tracking for Twitch channels  
- [SullyGnome](https://sullygnome.com/) — Twitch stats and analytics for streamers and games  
- [Streamscharts](https://streamscharts.com/) — Track statistics of live streams across Twitch, YouTube, Kick, Rumble, and other platforms  
- [TwitchDownloader](https://github.com/lay295/TwitchDownloader) — Download Twitch VODs, clips, and chat, with chat rendering and replay  

### Live Webcams
- [Windy Webcams](https://www.windy.com/webcams) — Map of live webcams worldwide, useful for checking conditions and activity at a location  

### Deepfake and Manipulated Media Detection
- [Deepware Scanner](https://deepware.ai/) — Tool to detect deepfakes in videos  
- [Sensity AI](https://sensity.ai/) — Commercial solution for deepfake and visual threat detection  
- [Deepfake-o-meter](https://zinc.cse.buffalo.edu/ubmdfl/deep-o-meter/landing_page) — Open platform from the University at Buffalo for testing media against multiple deepfake detectors
