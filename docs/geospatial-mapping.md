## 🛰 Geospatial & Mapping OSINT

[← Back to Index](../README.md)

### Satellite Imagery
- [Google Earth](https://earth.google.com/) — Global 3D imagery and satellite data  
- [Google Earth Pro](https://www.google.com/earth/versions/) — Desktop version with advanced tools; Google has announced the desktop app's retirement in 2027 (existing installs keep working, no further updates)  
- [Sentinel Hub](https://www.sentinel-hub.com/) — Access free Sentinel satellite imagery  
- [Copernicus Browser](https://browser.dataspace.copernicus.eu/) — Explore Sentinel imagery interactively (successor to the old Sentinel Playground, which now redirects here)  
- [EO Browser](https://www.sentinel-hub.com/explore/eobrowser/) — Browser-based access to Sentinel imagery  
- [NASA Worldview](https://worldview.earthdata.nasa.gov/) — Daily global satellite data  
- [NASA Earthdata Search](https://search.earthdata.nasa.gov/) — Search, visualize, and download NASA Earth observation datasets  
- [USGS EarthExplorer](https://earthexplorer.usgs.gov/) — Access historical satellite imagery  
- [ESA Copernicus Open Access](https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/explore-data) — Sentinel mission data access  
- [NOAA Satellite Data](https://www.nesdis.noaa.gov/) — US environmental satellite imagery  
- [Satellites.pro](https://satellites.pro/) — Online satellite maps viewer  
- [Mapbox Near-Time Imagery](https://www.mapbox.com/) — Near real-time satellite maps  
- [GIS Geography: 25 Satellite Maps](https://gisgeography.com/satellite-maps/) — Guide to alternative satellite maps  
- [Wayback Imagery](http://livingatlas.arcgis.com/wayback/) — Historic satellite imagery archive  
- [Maxar Discovery (now Vantor)](https://xpress.maxar.com) — High-resolution imagery archive; Maxar Intelligence rebranded as Vantor in October 2025  
- [Soar Atlas](https://soaratlas.com/) — User-uploaded and satellite imagery platform (formerly Soar.Earth, which now redirects here)  
- [OpenAerialMap](https://map.openaerialmap.org/) — Open collection of community-contributed aerial and drone imagery  
- [Google Earth Engine](https://earthengine.google.com/) — Planetary-scale geospatial analysis platform; free for academic and research use  

### Radar, Fire & Event Monitoring
- [ASF Data Search](https://search.asf.alaska.edu/) — Search and download SAR radar imagery, including Sentinel-1  
- [Umbra Open Data](https://umbra.space/open-data/) — Free SAR satellite imagery across 20+ global locations, no registration required  
- [NASA FIRMS](https://firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/) — Near-real-time active fire and thermal anomaly detections from satellites  
- [Copernicus Emergency Mapping](https://mapping.emergency.copernicus.eu/activations/) — On-demand satellite mapping from the Copernicus Emergency Management Service  
- [Zoom Earth](https://zoom.earth/) — Near-real-time satellite imagery, weather radar, and storm tracking  
- [Windy](https://www.windy.com/) — Weather forecast, radar, and satellite layers; useful for verifying weather conditions in a photo or video  
- [Global Nature Watch](https://globalnaturewatch.org/) — Forest and ecosystem monitoring data and tools (formerly Global Forest Watch)  

### Conflict & Event Mapping
- [ACLED](https://acleddata.com/) — Conflict monitor with data and analysis on political violence and protest worldwide (freemium)  
- [Liveuamap](https://liveuamap.com/) — Live map of conflicts, protests, and security events  

### Mapping and Street-level
- [Apple Maps](https://maps.apple.com/) — Map service by Apple  
- [Baidu Maps](https://map.baidu.com/) — Map service by Baidu  
- [Bing Maps](https://www.bing.com/maps) — Microsoft’s mapping solution  
- [GoKML](http://gokml.net/maps) — Online KML maps  
- [Google Maps](https://maps.google.com/) — Standard mapping and geolocation platform  
- [Google Street View](https://www.google.com/streetview/) — Ground-level panoramas worldwide  
- [Instant Streetview](https://instantstreetview.com) — Instant Google Street View  
- [Mapillary](https://www.mapillary.com/) — Crowdsourced street-level imagery  
- [Panoramax](https://www.panoramax.fr/) — Open, community-driven street-level imagery platform integrated with OpenStreetMap  
- [NearToilets](https://neartoilets.com/map) — Find clean public toilets  
- [OpenSeaMap](https://www.openseamap.org/) — Free nautical chart built on OpenStreetMap  
- [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/) — Community-driven world map  
- [Open Infra Map](https://openinframap.org/) — View of the world's electricity, telecom, oil, and gas infrastructure mapped in OSM  
- [HERE WeGo](https://wego.here.com/) — Mapping and routing platform  
- [Wikimapia](http://wikimapia.org/) — Collaborative map with user-added info  
- [Yandex Maps](https://yandex.com/maps/) — Map service by Yandex  
- [David Rumsey Map Collection](https://www.davidrumsey.com/) — Free archive of 150,000+ historical maps, hosted at Stanford University  

### OpenStreetMap Query & Data
- [Overpass Turbo](https://overpass-turbo.eu/) — Web-based tool to run Overpass queries on OpenStreetMap data and view results on a map  
- [Geofabrik Downloads](https://download.geofabrik.de/) — Daily-updated OpenStreetMap data extracts by region  
- [Overture Maps](https://overturemaps.org/) — Open, standardized map data from the Overture Maps Foundation  
- [uMap](https://umap.openstreetmap.fr/en/) — Create and share custom maps with OpenStreetMap layers  

### Location Estimation
- [GeoSpy.ai](https://geospy.ai) — Locates images through AI pixel analysis; used by law enforcement and government teams, demo access via request  
- [Picarta](https://picarta.ai) — Predicts a photo's location from architecture, landscape, and signage, no EXIF data required  
- [Findthatspot (SPOT)](https://www.findthatspot.io/) — Search for combinations of objects in public space using OSM data, to locate features for geolocation  
- [PeakFinder](https://www.peakfinder.com/) — Identify mountains and terrain from coordinates  
- [PeakVisor](https://peakvisor.com/) — Mountain identification and 3D terrain maps  

### Satellite and Aerial Tracking
- [Space-Track.org](https://www.space-track.org/) — US Space Command satellite data  
- [N2YO](https://www.n2yo.com/) — Real-time satellite tracking  
- [Heavens Above](https://heavens-above.com/) — Satellite, ISS, and astronomy tracking  
- [SatNOGS](https://satnogs.org/) — Open-source satellite ground station network  
- [Celestrak](https://celestrak.org/) — Orbital data for satellites and debris  
- [In-The-Sky Satellite Maps](https://in-the-sky.org/satmap_worldmap.php) — Interactive orbital maps  

### Sun vs. Shadow
- [ShadeMap](https://shademap.app/) — Sunlight and shadow simulation on an OSM-based map  
- [ShadowMap](https://app.shadowmap.org/) — Sunlight and shadow simulation on a 3D map  
- [SunCalc](https://suncalc.org/) — Calculate sun position and shadows for a given time  
- [SunEarthTools](https://www.sunearthtools.com/dp/tools/pos_sun.php) — Sun position and sun path tracking  
- [Bellingcat ShadowFinder](https://github.com/bellingcat/ShadowFinder) — Estimates the points on the Earth's surface where a shadow of a particular length could occur  

### Geospatial Analysis Tools
- [QGIS](https://qgis.org/) — Open-source desktop GIS application  
- [ArcGIS Online](https://www.arcgis.com/) — Cloud-based mapping and spatial analysis  
- [Gephi](https://gephi.org/) — Visualization and spatial network analysis  
- [Kepler.gl](https://kepler.gl/) — Web-based tool for visualizing large-scale geospatial datasets  
- [Felt](https://felt.com/) — Collaborative web mapping platform for sharing and annotating maps  
- [geojson.io](https://geojson.io/) — Browser-based editor for creating and viewing GeoJSON data
