## 🚢 Maritime OSINT

[← Back to Index](../README.md)

### Vessel Ownership & ID Records
- [Equasis](http://www.equasis.org/EquasisWeb/public/HomePage) — Vessel ownership and identification records, plus safety information on ships and companies (free registration required)  
- [IMO GISIS](https://gisis.imo.org/) — International Maritime Organization's database of ship and company particulars, port facilities, and casualty reports (free registration required)  

### Global Ship Tracking & AIS
- [MarineTraffic](https://www.marinetraffic.com) — Global ship tracking intelligence (AIS)  
- [VesselFinder](https://www.vesselfinder.com) — Ship positions and real-time AIS data  
- [VesselTracker](https://www.vesseltracker.com) — Vessel and port tracking using terrestrial and satellite AIS, with a ship database of about 170,000 vessels  
- [MyShipTracking](https://www.myshiptracking.com) — Live AIS vessel tracker  
- [AISHub](https://www.aishub.net/) — Free community-driven AIS data exchange with JSON/XML/CSV feeds  
- [ShipFinder](http://www.shipfinder.com) — Free satellite AIS ship tracking with live positions, ship details, and historical tracks  
- [ShipFinder.co](http://shipfinder.co) — Alternative ship tracking app by Pinkfroot, with vessel details and voyage playback  
- [Shipping Explorer](http://www.shippingexplorer.net) — Vessel positions and port data  
- [ShipSpotting](http://www.shipspotting.com) — Vessel photography and community, with 3.4 million+ photos  
- [CruiseMapper](http://www.cruisemapper.com) — Cruise ship tracker and itineraries  
- [Cruising Earth Ship Tracker](https://www.cruisingearth.com/cruise-ship-tracker/) — Cruise ship tracking tool (formerly Cruisin.me)  

### Sanctions, Shadow Fleet & Dark Fleet
- [FleetLeaks](https://fleetleaks.com/) — Free, daily-updated database of Russia-related sanctioned vessels from the US, EU, UK, Canada, Australia, and New Zealand, combined with live AIS tracking  
- [OpenSanctions Maritime Data](https://www.opensanctions.org/docs/maritime/) — Ships modelled as screenable entities (IMO number, MMSI, flag) linked to the companies that own, operate, and manage them  
- [OCCRP: How to Investigate Shadow Fleets](https://www.occrp.org/en/feature/practical-osint-for-investigating-shadow-fleets) — Practical OSINT guide (February 2026) on tracing a vessel's flag, ownership, and sanctions exposure  
- [TankerTrackers](https://www.tankertrackers.com/) — Independent maritime intelligence on crude oil shipments and storage, specializing in dark fleet tankers (commercial)  

### Specialized AIS & Regional Tools
- [BoatNerd AIS](http://ais.boatnerd.com) — AIS tracking (Great Lakes focus, needs JavaScript)  
- [AIS SK2HG](http://ais.sk2hg.se) — AIS tracking site (Bottenviken, Sweden)  
- [HELCOM AIS Map Service](https://maps.helcom.fi/website/mapservice/?datasetID=shipping203) — Baltic Sea shipping traffic and density data  
- [Marine Scotland AIS](https://marine.gov.scot/information/ais-shipping-traffic-average-weekly-density-vessel-types) — Scottish shipping AIS data  
- [Gloucester Harbour Trustees](https://gloucesterharbourtrustees.org.uk/ship-positions-marine-traffic/) — Local ship positions (links to an external live AIS map for the Sharpness area)  
- [EMODnet Human Activities](https://emodnet.ec.europa.eu/en/human-activities) — Single entry point for marine human-activity data in EU waters, including monthly vessel density maps  
- [MarineCadastre Vessel Traffic](https://hub.marinecadastre.gov/pages/vesseltraffic) — US portal explaining and linking to historical AIS vessel traffic data  

### Maritime Security & Port State Control
- [Paris MoU Inspection Search](https://www.parismou.org/Inspection-search) — Public search of a ship's port state inspection history, detentions, and risk profile (backed by the EMSA THETIS database)  
- [UKMTO](https://www.ukmto.org/) — UK Maritime Trade Operations centre, publishing verified maritime security alerts and incident reporting contacts  
- [ICC Commercial Crime Services (IMB Piracy Reporting Centre)](https://www.icc-ccs.org/) — Home of the 24-hour IMB Piracy Reporting Centre and its piracy map and reports  
- [MARAD MSCI](https://www.maritime.dot.gov/msci) — US Maritime Administration portal with government alerts and advisories on maritime security threats, such as in the Black Sea and Eastern Mediterranean  
- [NGA Maritime Safety Information](https://msi.nga.mil/) — US navigational warnings and maritime safety broadcasts (web app, needs JavaScript)  

### Cargo & Container Tracking
- [ShipmentLink](https://www.shipmentlink.com/servlet/TDB1_CargoTracking.do) — Carrier-specific cargo tracking  
- [ShipIt UK](https://www.shipit.co.uk/container-tracking.htm) — Container tracking service, now branded 1st Move International  
- [Searates Container Tracking](https://www.searates.com/container/tracking/) — Track containers on a live map by container number and shipping line  
- [track-trace](https://www.track-trace.com/container) — Container tracking across 169 carriers with automatic carrier detection  

### Fishing & Environmental Monitoring
- [Global Fishing Watch](https://globalfishingwatch.org/map) — Fishing vessel tracking and monitoring  
- [Skylight](https://skylight.global/) — AI-powered maritime awareness platform to detect illegal, unreported, and unregulated fishing (free for eligible government and NGO users)  
- [SkyTruth](https://skytruth.org/) — Nonprofit using satellite data to reveal environmental harm at sea, with free tools for advocates and journalists  

### Naval & Defense
- [Naval Technology](https://www.naval-technology.com) — Naval industry news and projects  
- [Janes](https://www.janes.com) — Defence and security intelligence, including analyst-verified OSINT on military equipment, installations, and units  
- [USNI News Fleet Tracker](https://news.usni.org/category/fleet-tracker) — Weekly approximate locations of US Navy carrier strike groups and amphibious ready groups  
- [Covert Shores](https://www.hisutton.com/) — Independent naval OSINT blog by H I Sutton on submarines, naval warfare, and special forces  

### Ports & Marine Info
- [Maritime Database](https://www.maritime-database.com) — Directory of official contact details for ports worldwide, with a map view  
- [Sea Distances](https://sea-distances.org/) — Port-to-port distance calculator  
- [Infomarine](https://www.infomarine.net/en/) — Greek and international shipping and marine news  
- [Marine Regions](https://www.marineregions.org/) — Standard reference of marine place names, areas, and maritime boundaries  

### Yachts & Luxury Vessels
- [SuperYachts.com](https://www.superyachts.com) — Superyacht news and database
