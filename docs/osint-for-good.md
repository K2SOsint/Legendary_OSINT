## 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 OSINT for Good (NGOs & Initiatives)

[← Back to Index](../README.md)

### Child Protection and Exploitation Prevention
- [NCMEC](https://www.missingkids.org/home) — US National Center for Missing and Exploited Children  
- [ICMEC](https://www.icmec.org/) — International Centre for Missing & Exploited Children, working on child protection policy, training, and cross-border cooperation  
- [Europol Trace an Object](https://www.europol.europa.eu/stopchildabuse) — Crowdsource identification of objects in the background of child abuse materials  
- [NCPTF](https://ncptf.org/) — National Child Protection Task Force  
- [FBI ECAP](https://www.fbi.gov/wanted/ecap) — FBI Endangered Child Alert Program  
- [Internet Watch Foundation](https://www.iwf.org.uk/) — UK charity that identifies and removes online child sexual abuse imagery and accepts anonymous reports  
- [Thorn](https://www.thorn.org/) — Nonprofit building digital tools to detect child sexual abuse material and speed up victim identification for law enforcement  

### Missing Persons
- [Trace Labs](https://www.tracelabs.org/) — Crowdsourced OSINT for missing persons  
- [Locate International](https://locate.international/) — UK charity that reviews cold cases and missing or unidentified person cases through volunteer OSINT investigators, free for families  
- [NamUs](https://namus.nij.ojp.gov/) — US national clearinghouse for missing, unidentified, and unclaimed person cases  
- [Missing Migrants Project](https://missingmigrants.iom.int/) — IOM database of migrant deaths and disappearances worldwide since 2014  

### Anti-Trafficking and Human Rights
- [Innocent Lives Foundation](https://www.innocentlivesfoundation.org/) — Identify perpetrators of child exploitation  
- [StopTheTraffik](https://www.stopthetraffik.org/) — Global initiative to disrupt human trafficking  
- [Polaris Project](https://polarisproject.org/) — Runs the US National Human Trafficking Hotline and publishes trafficking pattern research  

### Human Rights Evidence and Conflict Documentation
- [Amnesty Citizen Evidence Lab](https://citizenevidence.org/) — Tools and resources for human rights verification  
- [WITNESS](https://www.witness.org/) — Trains and supports people using video and technology to defend human rights, including work on deepfakes and AI-generated media  
- [Forensic Architecture](https://forensic-architecture.org/) — Research agency that reconstructs incidents of state and corporate violence using spatial and open source analysis (site needs JavaScript)  
- [Centre for Information Resilience](https://www.info-res.org/) — Open source investigations that document conflict harm and verify digital evidence  
- [DFRLab](https://dfrlab.org/) — Atlantic Council research center investigating disinformation, information operations, and digital rights, with a public Digital Sherlocks training program  
- [InformNapalm](https://informnapalm.org/en/) — Volunteer intelligence community publishing investigations on conflicts involving Russia and on disinformation  
- [Global Witness](https://www.globalwitness.org/en/) — Investigative organization exposing environmental destruction, corruption, and human rights abuses  

### Crisis Mapping and Humanitarian Data
- [Humanitarian OpenStreetMap Team](https://www.hotosm.org/) — Global mapper community producing open geospatial data for disaster response and crisis relief  
- [MapAction](https://mapaction.org/) — UK humanitarian charity deploying emergency mapping and data teams to disaster zones  
- [Humanitarian Data Exchange](https://data.humdata.org/) — OCHA's open platform for humanitarian datasets such as population, conflict, and displacement (page blocks automated access, but the platform is live)  
- [UNOSAT](https://unosat.org/) — UN satellite analysis programme producing maps and damage assessments for crises and disasters (site needs JavaScript)  
- [Ushahidi](https://www.ushahidi.com/) — Open-source platform that collects and maps crowdsourced reports for humanitarian and human rights work
