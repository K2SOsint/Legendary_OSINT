## ⚖️ Government & Legal Records

[← Back to Index](../README.md)

### Court and Legal Databases
- [PACER](https://pacer.uscourts.gov/) — US federal court records (paid access)  
- [RECAP](https://free.law/recap) — Free Law Project's browser extension and online archive of PACER documents, searchable via CourtListener  
- [CourtListener](https://www.courtlistener.com/) — Free access to US legal opinions and filings  
- [Caselaw Access Project](https://case.law/) — Harvard Law School's collection of historical US court decisions  
- [Cornell Legal Information Institute (LII)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/) — Free access to US Constitution, federal/state law, Supreme Court decisions, and a plain-language legal encyclopedia  
- [Justia](https://www.justia.com/) — US law and case database  
- [BAILII](https://www.bailii.org/) — British and Irish legal information institute  
- [legislation.gov.uk](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/) — Official home of UK legislation, managed by The National Archives  
- [EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/) — Access to European Union law  
- [CURIA](https://curia.europa.eu/) — Court of Justice of the European Union, with case law and judgments  
- [HUDOC](https://hudoc.echr.coe.int/) — European Court of Human Rights case-law database  
- [Rechtspraak.nl](https://uitspraken.rechtspraak.nl/) — Search Dutch court rulings  
- [wetten.overheid.nl](https://wetten.overheid.nl/) — Official Dutch government legislation portal  
- [Légifrance](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/) — Official French government portal for legislation and the Journal Officiel  
- [CanLII](https://www.canlii.org/) — Canadian case law and statutes  
- [AustLII](http://www.austlii.edu.au/) — Australian legal database  
- [WorldLII](http://www.worldlii.org/) — Free access to legal information from jurisdictions worldwide  

### Government Records and Open Data
- [Data.gov](https://www.data.gov/) — US government open data portal  
- [GovInfo.gov](https://www.govinfo.gov/) — US Government Publishing Office repository for congressional, legal, and executive branch documents  
- [Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/) — Searchable (unofficial) edition of US federal rules, proposed rules, and notices  
- [Regulations.gov](https://www.regulations.gov/) — US federal rulemaking dockets and public comments  
- [EU Open Data Portal](https://data.europa.eu/en) — European Union open datasets  
- [Eurostat](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat) — Official statistics and data on the EU and its member states  
- [data.gov.uk](https://www.data.gov.uk/) — UK government open data portal  
- [UK Data Service](https://ukdataservice.ac.uk/) — UK archive of economic, population, and social research data (academic, funded by UKRI/ESRC)  
- [data.overheid.nl](https://data.overheid.nl/) — Dutch government open data register  
- [World Bank Data](https://data.worldbank.org/) — Global development and economic datasets  
- [IMF Data](https://data.imf.org/) — International Monetary Fund data portal (moved here after the legacy imf.org/en/Data portal was retired in November 2025)  

### Public Procurement, Contracts & Lobbying
- [USAspending.gov](https://www.usaspending.gov/) — Official US federal spending data, including contracts and grants  
- [SAM.gov](https://sam.gov/) — US federal contracting, entity registration, and grant opportunities  
- [TED (Tenders Electronic Daily)](https://ted.europa.eu/en/) — EU public procurement notices from the Official Journal supplement  
- [EU Transparency Register](https://transparency-register.europa.eu/) — Register of organizations lobbying EU institutions, with their interests and resources  
- [FARA (US Department of Justice)](https://www.justice.gov/nsd-fara) — Foreign Agents Registration Act information, with a link to the public eFile database of registered foreign agents  

### Freedom of Information (FOI) Requests
- [MuckRock](https://www.muckrock.com/) — Nonprofit platform for filing and tracking public records requests  
- [AsktheEU.org](https://www.asktheeu.org/) — File access-to-documents requests with EU institutions  
- [FragDenStaat](https://fragdenstaat.de/en/) — Freedom of information portal for requests to German and EU authorities  

### Official Registers
- [OpenSanctions](https://www.opensanctions.org/) — Free consolidated search across 400+ global sanctions, PEP, and criminal-watchlist sources  
- [UN Sanctions List](https://main.un.org/securitycouncil/en/content/un-sc-consolidated-list) — Official UN Security Council consolidated sanctions list, available as PDF, XML, and HTML  
- [OFAC Sanctions List](https://sanctionssearch.ofac.treas.gov/) — US Treasury sanctions search  
- [UK Sanctions List](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-uk-sanctions-list) — Official UK sanctions designations; replaced the OFSI Consolidated List, which closed in January 2026  
- [US Consolidated Screening List](https://www.trade.gov/consolidated-screening-list) — Parties subject to US export, reexport, and transfer restrictions  
- [EU Sanctions Map](https://www.sanctionsmap.eu/) — Sanctions measures of the EU  
- [Interpol Red Notices](https://www.interpol.int/en/How-we-work/Notices/View-Red-Notices) — Wanted persons database  
- [FBI Most Wanted](https://www.fbi.gov/wanted) — FBI wanted fugitives list  
- [EU Most Wanted](https://eumostwanted.eu/) — Europe's most wanted fugitives, supported by Europol
